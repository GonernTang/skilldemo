## Clean Cloth for Placement

**Process:**
1. Navigate to the location where the cloth is visible.
2. Pick up the cloth from its current surface.
3. Navigate to a sinkbasin or bathtubbasin.
4. Execute 'clean [cloth] with [basin]' while holding the cloth.
5. Navigate to the target receptacle.
6. Move the cleaned cloth into the receptacle.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Attempting to clean a cloth without having it in your inventory first.
- Trying to clean items at locations other than sinkbasins or bathtubbasins.
- Placing the cloth in the target receptacle before cleaning it.

**Failure Recovery:**
- If the cloth is not found, check nearby cabinets or drawers for storage.
- If the basin is closed, open it before attempting to clean.

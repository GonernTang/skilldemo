## Heat Appliance Setup

**Process:**
1. Identify the correct heating appliance for the target object (e.g., mug goes in microwave or coffeemachine, pot goes in stove).
2. Navigate to the appliance and inspect its current state (open or closed).
3. If the appliance is closed, open it to access the interior.
4. Move the target object into the appliance's interior space.
5. Close the appliance door if the heating mechanism requires it to be sealed.
6. Execute the heat command only after confirming the object is inside and the appliance is ready.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Attempting to heat an object while holding it in your inventory rather than placing it inside the appliance first.
- Using incompatible heating devices, such as trying to heat a mug on a stoveburner instead of a microwave.
